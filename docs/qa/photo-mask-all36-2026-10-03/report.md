# Uploaded image masks — all 36 games

The game-owned photo correction is frozen in **21 files**: 18 photo-only CSS rules and identity/photo markers in Sports Siege, Arcade Deluxe and Bow Club. Uploaded portraits are strict circles with a thin thematic ring; transparent mascot silhouettes remain authored. No rules, physics, input or timers changed.

Bowling/Curling before originals reproduce rounded-square frames. Their fresh confirmation shows circular uploads. Bow Club additionally needed its TV identity to read the actual launcher roster because its engine snapshot omits avatar. Arcade Deluxe needed its photo-only object-fit override to win an existing contain rule.

## Actual coverage

All 36 catalog games reached actual live TV routes; **72 current TV originals** at 1280×720 and 1920×1080 are linked in the read-only review gallery. Two independent browser controllers used the full file-upload path; Bomb used three real controllers. Built-in bots supplied additional ordinary gameplay actors. Synthetic nonsquare four-colour files include 32×64 and 64×32. They are diagnostic uploaded-image fixtures, not personal camera photographs.

The first catalog run has 34 live pairs: Spy needed both actual role confirmations, and Hockey correctly rejected the odd five-player setup. Focused refreshes repaired the QA setup only. Latest pairs overlay that preserved packet. Exact unrelated source drift remains in the matrix; this is photo-renderer-scoped acceptance, not a claim of a globally frozen gameplay build.

## Verification

**1,305 evidence assertions pass**: 36 live route pairs, current owned source hashes, loaded 192×192 uploader outputs, square geometry, 50% crop, cover fit, thin rim, and 120 actual canvas photo draws with circular clips. **28 raster probes pass** across Bowling, Curling, Bow Club, Spy and Deluxe: all four coloured interior quadrants survive and diagonal corner samples exclude the uploaded pixels. A same-upload Bowling negative control detects coloured pixels in the old rounded-square corner and excludes them after the circle correction; interior pixels survive in both.

**16 shared originals** cover actual 32×64/64×32 upload previews, TV arrival and Our People at both TV sizes, browser Profile/Players/Rankings/profile drill-down at 402×874 and 320×568. Rankings rows use their existing compact design; actual profile drill-down shows the circular upload. Actual TEST_FAST engine results produce ranking history without seeded scores; accelerated result timing is not real-time gameplay validation. Shared sources had zero drift. All browser/server processes closed.

Every whole original was opened; exact image hashes are in `original-review.json`. Shared public/polish.css and public/game-art.js are root-owned and included in photo source freeze qualification.

## Limits

No physical device, camera or installed native build was tested. All36 controller portrait branches are source-audited; fresh controller game images and every result/human-winner state are not claimed. Sports Three scenes do not read uploaded-photo textures: the actual photo renderer is their DOM roster. Names/vehicles/markers and whole-body sumo/cowboy game actors retain authored presentation. Hidden score portraits have source-only coverage, explicitly recorded below.
