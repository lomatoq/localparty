# TV motion pass · 2026-10-05 (Claude "tv" lane)

Owner request: no hard cuts on the shared TV. Transitions between TV scenes
(lobby → game, game → results, results → lobby, pause/resume) and the way TV UI
enters and leaves should feel premium for kids and adults. Layout, geometry and
approved designs are unchanged.

## Motion language (tokens)

The tokens extend `motion.css` (`--motion-*`) and do not fork it. They are defined
in `tv-motion-20261005.css`, and the JS reads the same values.

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--tv-scene-close` | 380 ms (340 ms to lobby) | Doors travel in |
| `--tv-scene-hold` | 340 ms | Doors meet → doors part. The wordmark lands during this time. |
| `--tv-scene-open` | 460 ms | Doors travel out |
| `--tv-enter` | 420 ms | 10-foot UI entrance (header, cards, chips, notch, podium heading) |
| `--tv-exit` | 220 ms | UI leaving; faster than it came |
| `--tv-stagger` | 45 ms, capped at 10 items | Lists |
| `--motion-ease-travel` | `cubic-bezier(.77,0,.175,1)` | On-screen travel (doors closing) |
| `--motion-ease-out` (existing) | `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)` | Entrances, doors opening, exits |
| `--motion-ease-pop` (existing) | `cubic-bezier(.18,1.28,.35,1)` | Playful landings: wordmark, chips, resume pill |
| `--motion-ease` (existing) | `cubic-bezier(.2,.78,.2,1)` | Veil dissolves (pause, waiting, podium) |

Rules:
- Only transform and opacity are animated. No filter or backdrop blur is animated, and no new backdrop layer is added.
- The doors are the only large moving layers: two `will-change: transform` wrappers that exist only while the curtain is shown.
- Reduced motion uses crossfades only. The curtain fades in and out with the doors static. There is no push, stagger, flight or drop.
- Nothing replays on refresh. Lobby roster rows animate only for a name that was not on the list before.

## Transitions

**Lobby → game (and game → another game).**
- Before: tv.js swapped the DOM in the same frame that a 120 ms overlay fade began, so the waiting room showed through as the lobby vanished.
- Now, tv.js hands its render to `gate()`:
  1. Two wall-material doors close over the lobby. They are brick wall under a dark violet tint, with an italic 8° seam and a lime-into-lilac lit edge, and the doors give a small "thunk" overshoot as they meet.
  2. A seam flash plays.
  3. The new state renders behind the closed doors, at most 380 ms after it arrived.
  4. The game's own HD wordmark pops onto the seam over a soft halo.
  5. The doors part with a strong ease-out over the waiting room (r.1 also pushed the scene in from 1.04; r.2 dropped it for performance).
  6. The curtain wordmark flies into the waiting room's own wordmark position and scale, then hands over to the real (renderer-smoothed) logo.
  7. The title, hint, ready chips and count stagger in.
- Total is about 1.2–1.3 s, comparable to the old 1.16 s.

**Waiting → countdown/playing.**
- Before: the waiting room vanished in one frame.
- Now:
  - The waiting content lifts away (−18 px, 220 ms).
  - The waiting veil dissolves over 420 ms, revealing the field.
  - The match header drops in from above (centered notch) or slides in (rail and content caps), and its readouts follow.
- The real header element is never transformed while tv.js measures it; only `.tv-info-dock` is animated.

**Pause.**
- The veil dissolves in, then the PAUSE title springs in, then the hint.
- This replaces tv-show's generic fade. The approved pause surface is unchanged.

**Resume.**
- Before: a full opaque cover flashed over the game for about 560 ms.
- Now:
  - The pause card lifts away and the veil dissolves.
  - A compact "▶ RESUMING" pill pops over the live field and leaves about 0.8 s later.
- The field is never covered.

**Playing → results.**
- The podium presentation now covers quickly with a 240 ms dissolve, which hides the results relayout underneath, and settles from 1.03.
- Then the heading is choreographed: eyebrow, then wordmark (pop), then subtitle from the side, then the footer late.
- The plinth and crown choreography is unchanged.
- The match header leaves by retracting upward. This is a detached copy; the real bar leaves layout at once because tv.js sizes the game frame from it.

**Results/game → lobby.**
- The doors close over the receding podium or field, with the HeyPals logo on the seam.
- The doors part, and lobby content settles from the seam outward, nearest first, with no opacity flash.
- The header drops in last.

**Startup loader → lobby.**
- The loader copy lifts away while the lobby is already entering beneath it.
- Previously there was an empty wall between the fade and the entrance.

**Toasts.**
- `#notice` leaves with a short fade instead of vanishing. Its entrance is still motion.css's pop.
- Arrival cards were already animated.

Safety:
- A watchdog commits the render 780 ms after a gate if the close stalls.
- A 3.2 s fail-safe always removes the curtain.
- When the tab is hidden, the gate is skipped.
- `#tvSceneTransition` keeps its contract: visible during a transition, `has-logo` when the wordmark shows, hidden afterwards.

## Evidence (`.localparty-build/tv-motion-2026-10-05/`)

Harness: `scripts/capture-tv-motion.cjs`. It runs a real server with three real bots and real admin launch, force-start, pause, stop and results (Tap Race). Filmstrips are slow motion with `QA_SLOW=k`: WAAPI/CSS animations and page timers run k× slower, so one strip frame is about 30–80 ms of transition time. The caption on each frame is transition time.

| Folder | What |
| --- | --- |
| `before/` | True baseline before any change (Flappy, k=5), 720/1080. |
| `before-legacy/` | Same tree with `?motion=legacy` (Tap Race, k=8) + frame times. Hard cuts visible: e.g. `1280x720/stop-filmstrip.png` jumps podium → lobby in one frame. |
| `after/` | Revision .1 (k=8) + frame times. Superseded for performance by `after-r2/`. |
| `after-r2/` | **Current revision .2** (incl. Codex perf132 corrections, see below), 720 and 1080, all seven transitions + frame times. |
| `after-reduced/` | Reduced motion at 720: the doors fade in and out with no movement. |
| `timing-*-4k/` | 3840×2160 frame times, legacy vs revision .1. |
| `iter1/`, `iter2/` | Iterations: fixes for a double wordmark, empty-wall flash and an instant waiting exit. |

All strips listed in the report were opened and looked at.

Frame times, rAF in headless WebKit, no screenshots during measurement. Each cell is p95 / max in ms.

| Transition | legacy 720 | r2 720 | legacy 1080 | r2 1080 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| launch | 65 / 347 | 34 / 170 | 63 / 292 | 24 / 397 |

The max spikes come from launching the game process, loading the iframe and swapping the DOM, and they exist in legacy too.

Revision .1 was measurably heavier on return to the lobby: at 4K, stop p50 was 143 ms. It scaled the whole outgoing and incoming scene, which contain backdrop-filtered and masked layers, and it ran per-card settle layers. Revision .2 by Codex perf132 removed the full-scene scale and prepares the lobby settle while it is covered. That brings it back to legacy level. A 4K run for r2 was not repeated.

Tests:

## Revision .2 (Codex perf132, after an owner report on build125)

The owner reported curtains that stalled or looked vertical, and lag across surfaces. Codex perf132 then owned the performance corrections in these files and changed the following:
- The receding and pushing full scenes are removed. Only the doors move.
- The lobby settle is prepared while it is covered.
- The menu hero characters get their own entrance.
- A soft-logo startup dissolve and a startup grain were added.
- Resume is ignored while a scene curtain is running.

The tables above for `after-r2/` measure that current state. Hand-off, wordmark flight, door look and staging are unchanged.

## Not verified

- No physical Apple TV, AirPlay receiver or iPhone external display. All frames come from headless WebKit on macOS.
- Frame times are headless and relative (before vs after on the same machine), not device numbers.
- Slow-motion filmstrips scale WAAPI/CSS animations and page timers by k. Game iframes, canvas fireworks and the server run in real time, so game content advances faster than the transition in the strips.
- The destination-change path (stop and relaunch while the doors are closing) and the roster-row arrival were exercised in code review only, not in a captured strip.
