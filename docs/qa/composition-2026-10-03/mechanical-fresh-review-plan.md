Each of all36 rows needs current TV1920×1080 and native-controller phone402×874 originals, captured after actual game state settles. Record original SHA, sourceStart/sourceEnd hashes, phase/current player/control mode before and immediately after each original, and the new composition marker. ## Meaningful groups and state emphasis

- Arena/survival: active joystick/Throw/Fire, then Out separately where relevant; shared HUD must not mask authoritative movement or obstacles.
- Track/arcade: real Tap/Flap/steering/ball control, with progress/live feedback; lower roster and field are one intentional composition.
- Drawing/social: actual drawing canvas and tools, submission/off-turn, private prompt/role, vote/reveal; avoid leaking secrets on TV.
- Quiz: actual question and answer/reveal; evaluate complete question-answer cluster and roster balance rather than merely fitting four rectangles.
- Construction: actual selected support/pull or aligned crane/drop; field/title/rail axes and phone preview/force hierarchy.
- Tabletop: private hand/turn actions, current tile/Open or live striker; reserve community-card and board space appropriately.
- Sports/aim: real aim/release or Fire with swipe/touchpad surface; launch/gesture space remains functional and roster does not mask effects.

Review status is per game and surface: confirmed current composition, named defect, or unproven state/source. A coherent baseline topology can be preserved only with a reasoned new-original judgment. A clean mechanical scan cannot substitute for that judgment.
