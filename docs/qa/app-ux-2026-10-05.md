# App UX pass, 2026-10-05 (Claude lane "app")

This pass covers the phone app surfaces: the native host hub (partyapp native-shell), the guest controller (/play) and the native controller route (controller-bridge.js and tabs.js). Most of the work lives in two new files that load last:

- `public/app-ux-20261005.css`
- `public/app-ux-20261005.js`

Hooks into existing files:

- `public/index.html` and `public/native-shell/index.html`: one `<link>` and one `<script defer>` each, placed after `game-ui-polish-20261004.css`.
- `server.js`: two names added to the static whitelist, after the tv-motion entries.

These files were not touched: `background-scene.css`, `tabs.js`, `host.js`, `app.js`, the Pause/Lobby styles, header geometry, and the game, panel and TV files. Nothing was committed.

## Audit before changes (summary)

- The native game detail opened as a centered modal. It had no grabber and could not be dragged. It faded in from the center, with no link to the card that was tapped.
- The rule rows inside the detail ("Controls" / "How to win") were square strips with the text touching the edge. The design contract says grouped panels keep a full rounded contour.
- The guest Rules and Top sheets were placed inconsistently: Rules was centered, Room was bottom-anchored and Top floated mid-screen. The native controller bridge stretched Rules edge to edge.
- The native "Connecting the menu…" state was broken. The text was left-aligned on top of a ghost box, and the Reconnect button overlapped the box edge.
- Search with no results:
  - Native showed one left-aligned line of text and nothing else.
  - Guest showed "0 games" and an empty area, and the clear (×) floated under the search field instead of inside it.
- Host tab: in the browser fixture, a focus ring was painted around "Large QR" on every arrival. This did not show in the simulator baseline. The TV-browse status line used an upright face, unlike the italic hint right above it.
- Scrolling: the hero and the catalogue moved as one flat plane. Cards arrived from under the tab dock with no sense of depth.

## What changed and why

1. **Bottom sheets.** These dialogs are now floating sheets anchored to the bottom, at phone widths (≤640 px) only:
   - native `#gameDetail`
   - guest and native-controller `#rulesDialog`, `#statsDialog`, `#roomDialog`

   Details:
   - 28 px radius and a 10 px side inset.
   - In the iOS app the sheet sits just above the native floating tab bar (`--native-tab-reserve` + 8 px), because a web top-layer sheet cannot cover native chrome.
   - Entrance is a spring (linear(), damping 0.86, response 0.42 s) from below the edge. The exit leaves the same way in 180 ms, inside motion.js's existing 190 ms close window. The backdrop fades with it.
   - Under reduced motion the sheet uses a 160 ms opacity fade.
   - Centered confirms stay centered.
2. **Drag to dismiss.** The grabber pill and the whole sheet can be pulled down.
   - The sheet follows the finger 1:1, and dragging upward meets rubber-band resistance.
   - The backdrop lightens as you drag.
   - On release it dismisses if dragged past 32% of the sheet (capped at 170 px) or flicked faster than 0.45 px/ms. Otherwise it springs back.
   - Dismissing fires a light haptic through the existing bridge.
   - A drag that starts on a button does not also count as a tap.
   - Inner scrolling content keeps scrolling unless it is already at the top.
3. **Shared-element open (native hub).** The game art flies from the tapped card (or Host's Pick) into the detail sheet, on the sheet's own entrance clock.
   - It uses a top-layer popover clone, so the sheet does not clip it.
   - On close the art flies back into the card.
   - If you drag mid-flight, the hand takes over and the clone is removed.
   - The flight is skipped for keyboard opens and under reduced motion.
4. **Detail interior.** Rule rows are now rounded 18 px compact panels with 14 px inner padding, a 48 px summary height and 8 px gaps.
5. **Scroll depth** (WebKit 26+ scroll-driven animations, behind `@supports`; reduced motion is exempt).
   - The hub and home hero sinks at about ⅓ scroll speed and softens behind the sticky stack.
   - Catalogue grid cards are "dealt" as they rise from under the tab dock (translate 34 px, scale 0.955, opacity 0.35 → rest), only within the entry band.
   - Cards in reading position are untouched. All of this is compositor-only.
6. **States.**
   - Native loading: centered status and Reconnect, with a shimmer skeleton of the catalogue underneath.
   - Empty search on native and guest: a centered icon, the existing translated sentence, and a one-tap Clear button.
   - The guest × is now centered on the field.
7. **Host tab.** Arriving by touch focuses the panel title instead of the first button, so no ring is painted. The TV-browse status line uses the hint role (Fit Runner italic 400).
8. **Delight, kept small.**
   - Confetti bursts from Save Profile on a first join only. It is skipped for profile edits and under reduced motion.
   - Vote counters give a small spring pop when they change.

## Regression found and fixed

`tests/host-panel-browser.cjs` failed its deck-compaction timing assertion (class response 61–84 ms against a 55 ms limit). A/B testing showed the cause was a root-level `html:has(body.native-shell)` selector in my CSS: it forced style re-checks on every catalogue mutation.

I replaced it with a plain `body.native-shell` rule. After the fix the test passed 6 of 7 runs. The single failure was "geometry settles 308 ms > 300 ms", and the untouched baseline shows the same flake in 2 of 5 runs (316 ms and 321 ms).
