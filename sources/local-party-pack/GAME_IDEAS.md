# NEXT LOCAL PARTY GAMES

All concepts keep the same architecture: computer = shared screen, phones = controllers, one reusable QR / player identity system.

## 1. BOMB TAG — «ГОРЯЧАЯ БОМБА»
Joystick movement. One random player starts with a bomb. Touching another player passes it instantly. The fuse length is hidden and randomized, so nobody knows exactly when it explodes. Explosion costs a life/point, then a new round starts. Later rounds add bumpers, one-way gates and temporary safe islands. Extremely readable and creates panic immediately.

## 2. MAGNET HEIST — «МАГНИТНЫЕ ВОРЫ»
Joystick + one big PULSE button. Batteries/metal junk are scattered in the arena. Hold objects near your magnet and drag them to your goal. PULSE alternates polarity: one use pulls nearby objects/players, next use pushes them away. Heavy batteries are worth more but make you slower. You can steal another player's cargo at the last second.

## 3. LASER MIRRORS — «НЕ ТРОГАЙ ЛУЧ»
Every player controls one rotating mirror with a phone dial/joystick. A laser enters the arena and bounces between mirrors. Score when you redirect it into your colored target; lose a point if you burn another player's target or yourself. Every 15 seconds the source moves. This is spatial, cooperative for two seconds, then immediately treacherous.

## 4. DELIVERY PANIC — «НЕ УРОНИ ТОРТ»
Joystick. One fragile object sits in the middle. Players must carry it into their own delivery zone by physically pushing it, but hard impacts damage it. If it breaks, whoever caused the last big impact loses points. Moving doors, conveyor belts and wind zones change each round. More football/air-hockey than combat.

## 5. GHOST TRAFFIC — «ПЕРЕКРЁСТОК»
Each phone has only LEFT / RIGHT lane buttons. Everyone drives automatically through a ridiculous multi-lane intersection seen from above. Grab your colored passengers/packages and survive. Players can trigger traffic lights and lane switches, accidentally creating giant chain crashes. One-button chaos, very good for many players.

## 6. ECHO MAZE — «СЛЕПОЙ ЛАБИРИНТ»
Joystick + SONAR. The shared maze is almost completely dark. Your player sees only a tiny radius. Press SONAR to reveal walls and pickups for two seconds — but the pulse also reveals your position to a roaming monster and to other players. Reach the center / steal the relic / escape. Tension instead of pure arcade chaos.

## 7. ORBIT THIEVES — «НА ВЕРЁВКЕ»
Every player is physically tethered to the same heavy central ball. Joystick movement changes the whole group's orbit. Colored gems appear near the walls; you want your gems, but pulling toward yours drags everybody else away from theirs. If someone falls into a hazard, the tether yanks the rest. Very physical and visually stupid in the best way.

## 8. FLOOR IS LAVA: VOTE & RUN
Joystick for movement; every phone also gets three anonymous vote buttons between micro-rounds. The shared arena is made from tiles. Before each 12-second sprint, players secretly vote which tile type will disappear: red, circles, arrows, etc. Then everybody sees the result and scrambles. You can bluff and vote against the tile somebody else is standing on.

## 9. CROWN SNATCH — «КОРОНА»
Joystick. There is exactly one crown. Carrying it gives points every second but makes you slightly slower and leaves a bright trail. Touch the carrier hard enough to knock the crown loose. Map has short teleporters and bumpers. Match is continuous rather than round-based; the leader becomes an obvious target.

## 10. ONE SHOT WESTERN — «ДУЭЛЬ НА НЕРВАХ»
No joystick. Phone has one FIRE button. On the big screen everybody's character slowly circles a central table. Random fake cues happen: lamps flash, glasses fall, text appears. Only one cue is the real DRAW signal. Shoot before it = eliminated; react late = somebody else scores. Almost zero controls, maximum room reaction.

### Best next three to build
1. **BOMB TAG** — fastest to understand and likely the loudest party game.
2. **MAGNET HEIST** — adds a genuinely new physics mechanic and one extra button.
3. **ONE SHOT WESTERN** — radically different, needs almost no learning and scales to many players.
